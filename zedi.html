Zedi

A personal finance SaaS I built end to end, live at zedi.dev. Checking, cards, Venmo and receipts in one list, with nothing counted twice.

Rust Axum Next.js Postgres RLS SimpleFIN Claude Vision Stripe

Overview

Zedi grew out of an earlier finance agent I built for my own banking data in 2025. I restarted it as Zedi in January 2026, and in spring 2026 turned it into a multi-tenant SaaS through Cal Poly's Applied Human Factors course (IME 543), where usability testing shaped the product. It brings in statements or a read-only bank feed, reads receipts, sorts spending into categories, and shows where the money went.

The build

  • Rust + Axum API on PostgreSQL with row-level security and a two-pool tenancy model, so each user's data is isolated at the database layer.
  • Next.js frontend for the dashboard, transactions, accounts, analytics, receipts, and People views.
  • Statement import from CSV, QFX, and PDF. PDF statements are read by Claude and checked against their own printed balances; rows already seen are skipped, and card payments pair with the other side, so they don't count as spending or income.
  • Read-only bank sync through SimpleFIN on Pro. Zedi never sees the bank login and stores one read-only key, encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
  • Claude Vision receipt OCR: a photo becomes line items, and a receipt linked to its charge can split that charge by item category.
  • People ledger: split receipts with friends and keep a running who-owes-who balance per person. Venmo statement files tag payments to people.
  • Categorization with 450+ built-in rules, the user's own fixes, and optional AI.
  • Stripe billing for subscriptions, deployed on Railway.

Pricing

  • Free: unlimited CSV and QFX files, 10 receipt scans and 10 PDF scans a month, and opt-in AI categories for 50 rows a month.
  • Pro: $3.50 a month or $30 a year. Adds read-only bank sync (SimpleFIN bills its own $1.50 a month), unlimited scans, and unlimited AI categories.
  • Try Pro free for 30 days, with 50 receipt and PDF scans. No card to start. Bank sync in the trial still needs your own SimpleFIN subscription.
  • No ads, and data is never sold.
