PROJECT / ZEDI

Zedi

A personal finance web app I build, live at zedi.dev. Checking, cards, Venmo and receipts go in one place: upload the CSV, PDF or QFX statements your bank already gives you, or turn on read-only bank sync through Plaid on Pro. Card payments and Venmo transfers pair up when the match is clear, so they do not count as spending or income. Claude reads receipts line by line, and a People tab keeps one running balance with each friend.

Open zedi.dev Pricing

Free with your files: unlimited CSV and QFX imports, plus 10 receipt and 10 PDF scans a month. Pro is $5 a month or $48 a year and adds read-only bank sync through Plaid (up to 3 banks) and unlimited scans, with a 30-day free trial that asks for no card.

Built with Rust and Axum on Postgres row-level security, a Next.js front end, Claude for receipts and PDF statements, and an agentic Claude Code workflow.

Guides

What Zedi does for one specific job.

  • Split a bill or receipt by item with friends zedi.dev/for/split-receipts-with-friends

    Split any receipt by item, evenly or by percent. Tax is shared for you, the cents add up, and Zedi keeps a running tab with each friend. Free to start.

  • Track Venmo expenses: import your Venmo CSV zedi.dev/for/venmo-zelle-reconciliation

    Import your Venmo CSV next to your bank statements. Cash-outs and top-ups pair with the matching bank row and file as transfers, and friends’ payments tag to the people who sent them.

  • Track spending without linking your bank account zedi.dev/for/statement-import-without-bank-login

    Drop in the CSV, PDF or QFX file your bank already gives you. Copies are left out, rows are sorted on arrival, and uploading a file links nothing to your bank.

  • Itemize grocery receipts line by line zedi.dev/for/itemized-grocery-tracking

    A $247 Costco charge tells you nothing. Zedi reads the receipt, categorizes each product, and shows where the grocery money goes. Ten receipt scans a month free.

  • Import bank statement PDFs into a spending tracker zedi.dev/for/pdf-statement-import

    Some banks only give you a PDF. Zedi reads it for you, fixes flipped amount signs from the printed balances, leaves out copies and lines the rows up for your OK. Ten PDF statements a month free.

  • Reconcile bank statements without a spreadsheet zedi.dev/for/reconcile-bank-statements

    PDF statements checked against their printed balances, duplicates flagged, card payoffs and Venmo transfers paired, and the leftovers filed fast. No spreadsheet.

  • Split rent and bills with roommates, no spreadsheet zedi.dev/for/roommates

    Record each roommate’s share of rent and bills, split grocery receipts by item, and match Venmo and Zelle paybacks. One running balance per roommate.

  • A couple expense tracker for yours, mine and ours zedi.dev/for/couples

    Split costs your way: 50/50, by percent or item by item. Zedi keeps a running balance with your partner from receipts, Venmo and amounts you write down.

  • A private bank statement analyzer for your spending zedi.dev/for/analyze-bank-statements

    Thinking of pasting a bank statement into a chatbot? Zedi reads PDF, CSV and QFX statements into your own private records, sorted, with copies left out.

  • Split a restaurant bill by item, tip included zedi.dev/for/split-restaurant-bill

    Snap the check: Claude reads each dish, the tax and the tip, handwritten tips too. Split by item or evenly, and Zedi keeps the tab until friends pay up.

  • How to split groceries with roommates, by item zedi.dev/for/split-groceries-with-roommates

    Four ways roommates split groceries, and the one that ends the arguments: split the receipt by item, tax included, with a running balance per roommate.
