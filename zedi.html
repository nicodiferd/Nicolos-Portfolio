PROJECT / ZEDI

Zedi

A personal finance web app I design and build, live at zedi.dev in open beta. Checking, cards, Venmo and receipts land in one list, counted once: upload the statements your bank already gives you, or turn on read-only bank sync on Pro.

Open zedi.dev Pricing

Built with Rust and Axum on Postgres row-level security, a Next.js front end, Claude for receipts and PDF statements, and a Claude Code multi-agent workflow.

Guides

What Zedi does for one specific job.

  • Split a receipt by item zedi.dev/for/split-receipts-with-friends

    Split any receipt by item, and Zedi keeps a running balance of who owes you. Venmo and Zelle paybacks clear the oldest open share first. Free, from files you upload.

  • Track Venmo expenses: import your Venmo CSV zedi.dev/for/venmo-zelle-reconciliation

    Import your Venmo CSV next to your bank statements. Cash-outs and top-ups pair with the matching bank row and file as transfers, and friends’ payments tag to the people who sent them.

  • Track spending without linking your bank account zedi.dev/for/statement-import-without-bank-login

    Drop in the CSV, PDF or QFX file your bank already gives you. Copies are left out, rows are sorted on arrival, and uploading a file links nothing to your bank.

  • Itemize grocery receipts line by line zedi.dev/for/itemized-grocery-tracking

    A $247 Costco charge tells you nothing. Zedi reads the receipt, categorizes each product, and shows where the grocery money goes. Ten receipt scans a month free.

  • Import bank statement PDFs into a spending tracker zedi.dev/for/pdf-statement-import

    Some banks only give you a PDF. Zedi reads it for you, fixes flipped amount signs from the printed balances, leaves out copies and lines the rows up for your OK. Ten PDF statements a month free.

  • Reconcile bank statements without a spreadsheet zedi.dev/for/reconcile-bank-statements

    PDF statements checked against their printed balances, duplicates flagged, card payoffs and Venmo transfers paired, and the leftovers filed fast. No spreadsheet.

  • Split bills with roommates and track who owes what zedi.dev/for/roommates

    One running balance with each roommate: rent you covered, groceries split by item, Venmo paybacks matched to the right person. Free, from files you upload.

  • Track shared spending as a couple zedi.dev/for/couples

    See what you both really spend across bank, card and Venmo files, split receipts into yours and mine, and keep a running tab with your partner. Free, no ads.

  • A private bank statement analyzer for your spending zedi.dev/for/analyze-bank-statements

    Thinking of pasting a bank statement into a chatbot? Zedi reads PDF, CSV and QFX statements into your own private records, sorted, with copies left out.
