Flora

Year-long senior capstone with Bloomfilter, improving HCI for AI-agent integration inside project-management tools. Winner of Best in Lab at Cal Poly.

Best in Lab HCI AI Agents Research Enterprise

Overview

Flora was my senior capstone at Cal Poly, a year-long project with Bloomfilter, mentored by Dr. Awwad. The brief: as AI agents move into enterprise project-management software, the interface layer between humans and those agents is where adoption succeeds or fails. We spent the year researching and designing that layer.

The problem

AI tooling is moving faster than the interfaces people use it through. Project managers don't want another dashboard; they want assistance inside the tools they already live in, with enough transparency to trust what the agent is doing. The question was how to design that assistance so people actually adopt it.

What I worked on

HCI design for AI-agent integration in project-management tools, focused on increasing user adoption in enterprise software.

Outcome

Our team capped the year by winning Best in Lab at the senior project expo. The detailed work belongs to Bloomfilter, so this page stays high level.
